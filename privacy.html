Privacy

Card Vault has no server, no account and no analytics. The app is a set of static files; once loaded, it works offline.

What is stored, and where

  • Your cards and gift vouchers are stored in this browser's local storage, encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • The encryption key is protected by your 6-digit PIN (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds), and optionally by a fingerprint passkey and a backup password. The PIN itself is never stored.
  • Theme, auto-lock time and the salts needed to unlock are stored unencrypted. They contain no card data.

What leaves the device

Nothing, unless you export a file. Encrypted backups can only be opened with your backup password. The "readable JSON" export is plain text; treat it like a printout of your cards.

Fingerprint unlock

Fingerprint unlock uses a passkey created by your phone. The fingerprint never reaches the app; the phone only releases a key after it has checked you.

Limits

Anyone who knows your PIN, or has your unlocked phone with the app open, can see your cards. Someone who copies the browser's storage could try all one million 6-digit PINs offline, so keep your phone's own screen lock on and do not use Card Vault on shared computers.
